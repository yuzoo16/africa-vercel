Tanzania has one of Africa's strongest combinations of wildlife destinations. The classic northern circuit links Tarangire, Ngorongoro and Serengeti, while longer journeys can add Lake Manyara, the southern parks, Kilimanjaro or Zanzibar. Tanzania's tourism board continues to promote Tarangire, Ngorongoro and Serengeti as a signature northern safari circuit.

Short Tanzania Safari Packages

2-Day Tanzania Safari

Route: Arusha → Tarangire or Ngorongoro → Arusha

A two-day safari is designed for travelers with very limited time. Tarangire works particularly well when elephants, baobabs and a relatively compact wildlife experience are the priority, while Ngorongoro provides a concentrated crater game-drive experience.

Best for: Short holidays, travelers connecting from Zanzibar, business travelers and first-time visitors with only a weekend available.

Accommodation: Budget to Luxury.

Important: Two days allows very little flexibility for weather, road conditions or wildlife viewing.

3-Day Tanzania Safari

Route: Arusha → Tarangire → Ngorongoro → Arusha

This is one of the most practical short northern-circuit itineraries. Tarangire provides a different landscape and elephant experience before the journey continues toward the Ngorongoro highlands and crater.

Best for: First-time visitors with limited time.

3-Day Serengeti Safari

Route: Arusha → Serengeti → Serengeti → Arusha

Designed specifically for travelers whose priority is the Serengeti rather than visiting several parks. Depending on the season, the itinerary can focus on central, western, northern or southern Serengeti.

FAQs

How many Tanzania packages do you offer?

This index lists all 50 current Tanzania itineraries, from short expresses to grand tours. Every one is private and customizable.

What does a Tanzania safari cost?

See per-package from-prices on the cards above (green-season baselines, park fees included). Final quotes depend on season, camp tier and group size - always line-itemed.

Tanzania's cost structure splits in two: the northern circuit (Tarangire, Ngorongoro, Serengeti) carries the country's highest daily tariffs - park and concession fees alone reach US$70-120 per adult per day - while southern and western parks (Nyerere, Ruaha, Mahale) are cheaper per day but add flight legs of US$250-450. Mid-range private safaris commonly land at US$350-600 per person per day all-in.

How far ahead should we book Tanzania?

Migration and permit seasons 6-12 months ahead; green-season value trips 2-4 months. Camp and flight availability - not price - is the real constraint.

Can we combine Tanzania with other countries?

Routinely: Kenya plus Tanzania, safari plus Zanzibar or Diani, Uganda plus Kenya. Ask for multi-country routings in one proposal.

Are these trips private?

Yes - every package runs with your own vehicle and guide on your dates. Group departures are listed separately where available.

What is NOT included that surprises first-timers?

International flights, visas, travel insurance (required), premium drinks, the balloon flight where offered, and tips. Our quotes line-item park fees and permits so the total you compare is the total you pay.

What happens if it rains?

Game drives run rain or shine - vehicles have roofs and ponchos, animals remain active, and green-season light is superb for photography. Only true downpours pause activity, and camps flex schedules around them.

What insurance do we need?

Comprehensive travel insurance is required, including medical evacuation (AMREF/Flying Doctors cover, about 30 dollars, is recommended for remote circuits). Bring policy details - camps and operators ask for them.

Can we customise the route?

Freely - add nights, upgrade camps, swap parks or append beach and trekking. Request a proposal and we re-quote line by line.
