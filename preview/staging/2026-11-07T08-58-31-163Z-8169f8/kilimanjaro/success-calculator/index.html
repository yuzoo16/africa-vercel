Kilimanjaro success estimator

Answer honestly. You get an educational estimate of difficulty and preparedness plus route and acclimatization advice. It is not a scientific prediction and never a summit guarantee - weather, illness and individual altitude response vary.

How to read your result

Acclimatization profile beats fitness: 8-day Lemosho succeeds around 90% with quality crews; rushed 5-day Marangu near 70%. Extra days are the cheapest summit insurance in trekking. Whatever your score: train hills with a pack 8-12 weeks out, arrive 2 days early, hydrate aggressively, and tell your guide every symptom immediately.
