Same summit, different first halves: Lemosho approaches from the scenic west, Machame climbs the classic southern line. Success, crowds and cost separate them.

Success rates

Eight-day Lemosho near 90 percent; seven-day Machame near 85 percent; six-day versions of either fall toward the high 70s. Days beat fitness - the route that gives you eight wins.

Scenery and crowds

Lemosho traverses forest, Shira plateau, Lava Tower and Barranco - the full transect with moderate traffic. Machame is steeper early, equally dramatic late, and the busiest route on the mountain.

Cost

Eight-day Lemosho from about 3,400 dollars; seven-day Machame from about 2,950. One extra day buys the best odds in trekking - cheap insurance.

Verdict

First-timers and photographers: Lemosho 8-day. Social trekkers on a budget: Machame 7-day. Shoulder-season solitude seekers: consider Rongai. Run the estimator with your profile.

FAQs

Which route is easier?

Lemosho gradients are gentler and longer; Machame is steeper early. Neither is technical - Barranco Wall is a scramble with guides spotting.

Is Machame too crowded?

It is the busiest route, noticeably at Machame and Shira camps. Early starts and good guides mitigate it; Lemosho and Rongai are quieter.

Can beginners do either?

Yes with the longer format and hill training. Eight-day Lemosho is the single best beginner choice on the mountain.

What is the real cost difference?

About 400-500 dollars join-in: one extra park-fee day plus crew day. Private climbs widen the gap.

The real difference between a US$250 day and a US$600 day is where you sleep and who is with you: camp position relative to wildlife, vehicle-to-guest ratios, guiding depth and how many other vehicles share your sighting. Between US$150 and US$300 the gains are real but incremental; above US$600 you are mostly paying for exclusivity and staff-to-guest ratios rather than better sightings.

Which photographs better?

Lemosho: Shira, Lava Tower and Barranco in uncrowded light. Machame matches it from day three onward.

How do we decide?

Budget plus 7 days: Machame. Best odds plus scenery: Lemosho 8-day. Rainy months: Rongai. Short on time: neither - wait until you have the days.

Do routes share camps?

They converge at Barranco/Barafu for summit night. Early days differ completely - that is where Lemosho earns its premium.

Which has better guides?

Crew quality depends on operator, not route. Verify KPAP-standard wages and summit-night ratios in writing.

Add safari after?

Always if days allow: 5-day safari legs recover on game drives.
