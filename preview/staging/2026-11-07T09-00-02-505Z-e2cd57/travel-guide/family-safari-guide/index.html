Family Safari Guide: Ages, Parks and Pacing

Kids remember safaris forever if pacing respects them: pools at every stop, walking and boating breaks between drives, private vehicles for nap-and-snack flexibility.

Ages that work

Six plus suits East Africa fly-drive circuits; four plus works in malaria-free South African reserves. Gorilla trekking is strictly 15-plus; walking safaris usually 16-plus; balloon flights usually 7-plus.

Malaria-free options

Madikwe, Pilanesberg and Eastern Cape reserves offer Big Five without prophylaxis debates. Elsewhere, pediatric prophylaxis per your doctor plus nets and repellent manage risk well.

Pacing and rooms

Private vehicle always. Two-night stays minimum per stop. Family tents and interconnecting rooms at most lodges - request early, inventory is limited. Naivasha boats, Crescent Island walks and cultural mornings break up drive days beautifully.

FAQs

What age is best for a first safari?

Six to eight for East Africa; four-plus for malaria-free South Africa. Under-fours face lodge restrictions and long-drive limits.

Can teens trek gorillas?

At fifteen and above, yes - a superb older-teen milestone. Under-15s get chimps, savanna and forest walks instead.

The number behind the rule: gorilla visits run on IUCN monitoring standards set at 15, and Uganda and Rwanda both enforce it at permit purchase with passport checks - no discretion at the trailhead. Teens who cannot trek can still take Kibale chimps (from 12), community walks and Queen Elizabeth boat cruises, which cover a full trekking-day schedule.

How do we handle malaria with kids?

Pediatric formulations exist for most prophylaxis regimes; combine with nets, long sleeves and evening repellent. Malaria-free reserves remove the question entirely.

Can we customise the route?

Freely - add nights, upgrade camps, swap parks or append beach and trekking. Request a proposal and we re-quote line by line.

Do we need a visa?

Most visitors need one: Tanzania offers visas on arrival for many nationalities, Kenya uses an eTA applied for online before travel, Uganda an e-visa. We confirm the current rule for your passport before you fly.

One decision per country, and they differ. Tanzania sells visas on arrival for most passports - US$50, US$100 for United States citizens - or by eVisa in advance. Kenya has issued only the eTA since 2024, at US$32.50 online at least three days before departure. Uganda runs an e-visa printed before you fly, and the US$100 East African Tourist Visa folds Kenya, Uganda and Rwanda into a single stamp. Six months of passport validity covers all three. Send us passport details at proposal stage and we confirm every rule for your nationality before you commit to flights.

Traveling solo - how do single supplements work?

All itineraries run solo. Expect a 20 to 50 percent supplement where lodges charge per room; vehicles cost the same whether one or six ride. Ask for the honest supplement math per trip.

Is a balloon flight worth adding?

Once, yes: a dawn flight over the Serengeti or Mara with a bush breakfast runs 550 to 600 dollars and minimum ages apply. Book at proposal stage - seats sell out months ahead in season.

How far ahead should we book?

Peak Migration and gorilla-permit months need 6 to 12 months. Green season often works with 2 to 4 months notice.

Is this trip suitable for children?

With a private vehicle and pools at stops, yes for most routes. Gorilla trekking has a strict minimum age of 15.
