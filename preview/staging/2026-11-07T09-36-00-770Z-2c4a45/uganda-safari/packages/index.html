FAQs

How many Uganda packages do you offer?

This index lists all 4 current Uganda itineraries, from short expresses to grand tours. Every one is private and customizable.

What does a Uganda safari cost?

See per-package from-prices on the cards above (green-season baselines, park fees included). Final quotes depend on season, camp tier and group size - always line-itemed.

Uganda's middle is East Africa's middle - roughly US$300-500 per person per day for private mid-range - but gorilla trekking changes the arithmetic: permits run US$800 per person (Rwanda US$1,500), so a three-day Bwindi trip can carry US$1,600 of permits for two before any lodging. Chimpanzee permits at Kibale (around US$250 in high season) and Queen Elizabeth park fees follow the same line-item logic.

How far ahead should we book Uganda?

Migration and permit seasons 6-12 months ahead; green-season value trips 2-4 months. Camp and flight availability - not price - is the real constraint.

Can we combine Uganda with other countries?

Routinely: Kenya plus Tanzania, safari plus Zanzibar or Diani, Uganda plus Kenya. Ask for multi-country routings in one proposal.

Are these trips private?

Yes - every package runs with your own vehicle and guide on your dates. Group departures are listed separately where available.

What is NOT included that surprises first-timers?

International flights, visas, travel insurance (required), premium drinks, the balloon flight where offered, and tips. Our quotes line-item park fees and permits so the total you compare is the total you pay.

What happens if it rains?

Game drives run rain or shine - vehicles have roofs and ponchos, animals remain active, and green-season light is superb for photography. Only true downpours pause activity, and camps flex schedules around them.

What insurance do we need?

Comprehensive travel insurance is required, including medical evacuation (AMREF/Flying Doctors cover, about 30 dollars, is recommended for remote circuits). Bring policy details - camps and operators ask for them.

Can we customise the route?

Freely - add nights, upgrade camps, swap parks or append beach and trekking. Request a proposal and we re-quote line by line.
