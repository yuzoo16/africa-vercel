Wildlife: effectively equal, differently staged

Same species, same herds. The Mara's density means shorter drives between sightings in season; the Serengeti's scale stages predator-prey drama (calving, Grumeti) the Mara never hosts. Resident cats are superb in both year-round - lions, leopards and cheetahs do not migrate.

Migration timing decides

January to June: Serengeti only - the Mara has no herds. July to October: both, with the Mara concentrating crossings and the northern Serengeti offering wilder, emptier river frontage. November to December: herds return south; Serengeti again. See the month-by-month calendar.

Crowds and concessions

The Mara's reserve roads punish shared vehicles in season - twenty minibuses per crossing. Mara conservancies (Naboisho, Olare Motorogi, Mara North) fix this with bed limits, off-road rights and night drives. The Serengeti's scale disperses crowds naturally, except Seronera in August. Private vehicles plus smart zones beat reputation every time.

Cost comparison

Kenya typically runs 10-15 percent less for equivalent tiers, mostly on park fees and shorter distances. Tanzania's crater day and Zanzibar combos add value differently - compare line-item quotes, not headlines. Full breakdown: safari cost guide.

How to choose

  • Traveling Jan-Jun? Serengeti - the Mara has no herds.
  • Jul-Oct first safari? Either; Mara is logistically easier, Serengeti north is wilder.
  • Honeymoon or photography? Whichever offers a private concession in budget.
  • Budget? Kenya edges cheaper; Tanzania's combos add value differently.

FAQs

Can I see the Migration in both?

July to October, yes. Outside those months only the Serengeti holds herds - the Mara relies on resident game, still excellent for cats.

Which is cheaper?

Which is better for photography?

The Mara for density and short drives; the Serengeti for calving action and empty river frontage. Conservancy access matters more than the border.

Can we do both in one trip?

Yes via Isebania in 10-12 day combos, best July to October. Off-season, going deep in one beats splitting.

Which has fewer crowds?

The Serengeti by scale, except Seronera; the Mara by conservancy choice. Avoid reserve-gate corridors and minibus hours in both.

Which is better for families?

Kenya edges it: shorter drives, Naivasha activity breaks, Mara fly-ins. Tanzania wins from age eight up with pools and a Zanzibar finish.

Do the same animals live in both?

Yes - one ecosystem. Resident lions, leopards, elephants and rhinos stay put; only the wildebeest, zebra and gazelle herds migrate.

How many days in each?

Three nights minimum per side: two locations in three nights beats one base. Seven days total suits one side deeply.

Should we add the crater or Amboseli?

The crater pairs only with the Serengeti side; Amboseli only with the Mara side. Both add a distinct ecosystem day - take it if days allow.
