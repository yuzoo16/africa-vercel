Calving (Jan-Mar): predators and value

Ndutu and the southern Serengeti: 8,000 calves daily, lions and cheetahs hunting in daylight, few vehicles, lower rates. The connoisseur's season and the best photography value in Africa.

Grumeti (Jun): exclusivity

Western corridor crossings with a fraction of Mara-season vehicles. Remote camps, dramatic river action, superb cats - for second-timers and photographers avoiding crowds.

Mara crossings (Jul-Oct): peak drama

Northern Serengeti and Masai Mara: herds bunch at crossing points for days, then cross in minutes. Three-plus nights near Kogatende with picnic lunches; patience is the strategy. August-September peak.

Shoulder months (Nov-Dec, Apr-May)

Herds transit via Seronera; resident game carries the trip; rates drop 20-35 percent. April-May long rains limit some roads - ask about routing.

The one rule

Camp location beats luck. Mobile camps near the herds see ten times the action of lodges two hours away. See month-by-month positions and timed safaris.

FAQs

Which month has the most dramatic crossings?

August to September at the Mara River, with July building and October trailing. Book Kogatende camps 9-12 months ahead.

Is calving really as good as crossings?

For sustained action, better: weeks of hunts versus hours of waiting. Photographers and returning visitors overwhelmingly prefer February.

What if rains shift the herds?

Itineraries built on two zones (e.g. central plus north) insure against timing shifts. Single-zone short trips carry the most timing risk.

How many nights for crossings?

Three minimum near the river, four-plus in August. Day-trippers from distant lodges see crossings by luck alone.

Kenya or Tanzania for the Migration?

Same herds: Tanzania holds them ten months with more variety; Kenya concentrates them July-October with easier logistics.

Are crossings guaranteed?

Never on a given day. Herds bunch, hesitate, sometimes turn back. Three-plus nights converts odds into probability.

What does Migration season cost?

Peak premiums of 15-25 percent over green season; calving months price between. Mobile camps cost more than lodges but position better.

Can kids enjoy Migration trips?

Yes with realistic briefings: waiting days bore under-8s. Calving season suits families better than crossing vigils.

Balloon during Migration?

Superb: dawn flights over herds with bush breakfast. Book at proposal stage - 550 to 600 dollars, seats limited.
