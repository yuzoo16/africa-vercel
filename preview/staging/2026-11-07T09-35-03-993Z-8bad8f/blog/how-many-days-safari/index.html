Why the last two days add most

Days 1-2 acclimatize; days 3-5 deliver icons; days 6-plus deliver patience - full-day crossing waits, second chances on leopards, rest that sharpens eyes. Rushed trips see parks; unhurried trips see behavior.

Adding beach, trekking, cities

Zanzibar/Diani: plus 3-5 nights. Kilimanjaro: plus 7-9 days. Cape Town: plus 3-4 nights. Gorilla permits: fixed dates anchor the whole itinerary - book them first.

FAQs

Is 4 days enough for Tanzania?

As a taste: Tarangire, crater, one Serengeti day with a long transit. Five transforms it with a second Serengeti day.

How long for gorillas plus savanna?

Seven minimum:chimps, lions and gorillas. Three covers a fly-in trek only.

The arithmetic: one night Entebbe or Kigali, two for Kibale and Queen Elizabeth, two for Bwindi, one buffer, one for the exit flight - seven is the floor, nine the comfortable version. Road-only Uganda wants ten. Gorilla-only fly-in (Kisoro airstrip, two nights) still satisfies the itch, but the savanna is what makes the trip feel like a safari.

Do travel days count?

We quote park days honestly: arrival and departure days are logistics, not safari. Ask any operator which days include game drives.

Longer or more luxurious?

Longer, almost always: two extra mid-range days beat one suite night for sightings. Upgrade tier after duration is right.

How many beach nights?

Three minimum, four to five ideal. Two is a tease after safari dust.

Can we split two countries in 10 days?

Kenya plus Tanzania works at 10 (not 7): Mara, Serengeti, crater with border handling. Shorter splits waste days in transit.

What about jet lag?

Add a pre-night after long-haul arrivals. Day-one game drives after red-eyes underperform - rest first.

School-holiday constraints?

June-August and December-January align with dry seasons in East Africa - book 9-12 months ahead for family rooms.

Ask before booking?

Which days are full game-drive days, where are overnights, and what is driving versus flying time daily. Vague answers hide windscreen safaris.
