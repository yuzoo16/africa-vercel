Why visit Botswana

Botswana deliberately limits beds, so concessions the size of small countries host a handful of camps. The result: the Okavango's mokoro (dugout canoe) safaris past elephants, Chobe riverfront herds of thousands, wild dogs in Moremi and Linyanti, and meerkat mornings on the salt pans. The honest catch: it costs more — remote camps fly in staff and supplies. We balance 1–2 signature water camps with fairer-priced Chobe/Maun bases so the trip stays exceptional without fantasy pricing.

FAQs

Why is Botswana more expensive?

Remoteness plus deliberate low-volume policy: camps fly in supplies and staff and pay high concession fees for huge private areas. Mobile tented safaris moderate the bill without cutting wildlife.

Botswana's concession fees fund the lowest vehicle densities in Africa - a private Okavango block can charge US$50-150 per guest per night in park and concession levies alone. Add flown-in supplies, staff rotations and seasonal demand peaks, and the per-day floor sits near US$600 before luxury tiers. Mobile tented safaris cut the bill by moving camps seasonally and sharing the same wilderness.

When is the Delta flooded?

Angolan rains arrive May to August - peak water and mokoro season is June to September, even though local rains ended months earlier.

What is a mokoro safari?

A silent poled dugout canoe gliding reed channels past hippos and elephants, with walks on palm islands after. No engine, no rush.

Chobe or Moremi for a short trip?

Chobe for riverfront herd masses by boat; Moremi and Khwai for predators, wild dogs and concession freedoms.

How many days does Botswana need?

Five for Delta plus Chobe; eight for three camps; ten to twelve with Victoria Falls or Makgadikgadi pans.

Can we combine Victoria Falls?

Easily: Kasane to the Falls is a 90-minute transfer. Two nights covers the Falls walk, sunset cruise and optional helicopter.

Is Botswana a family destination?

For older children, superb: mokoro safaris and walks (minimum ages usually 12-plus). Malaria prophylaxis required.

What about green season?

November to April brings the zebra migration, dramatic skies and lower rates - a connoisseur's season.

How do internal flights work?

Light aircraft hop camp to camp with strict 20kg soft-bag limits. Camp availability, not price, is the constraint.
