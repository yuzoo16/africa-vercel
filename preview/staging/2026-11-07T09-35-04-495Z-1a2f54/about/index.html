About Adventure Safari Africa

We are a specialist African safari company designing private, tailor-made safaris across Tanzania, Kenya, Uganda, Botswana, Namibia, South Africa and Ethiopia. One specialist owns your trip from first email to wheels-down: real itineraries, named camps, line-item pricing, Migration-timed positioning and honest answers - including when to wait, spend less, or go elsewhere.

Our story

Adventure Safari Africa grew out of guiding in northern Tanzania, where our founder watched two realities side by side: travelers experiencing the trip of a lifetime, and neighboring communities seeing too little of its value. The company was built to close that gap - a safari operation whose itineraries, hiring and profit-sharing keep value where the wildlife lives.

Our mission

To design Africa's most honestly planned private safaris while directing tourism income - through wages, community camps and structured giving - to children, communities and conservation.

Why we exist

Because the safari industry too often extracts: foreign-owned trucks, minimum-wage crews, parks as backdrops. We exist to prove the alternative is also the better trip - local experts, fair pay, community camps with superior positioning, and travelers who know exactly where their money lands.

Our safari philosophy

  • Private by default: your vehicle, guide and schedule on every trip.
  • Positioning over luck: camps chosen by month, herd and light - not brochure.
  • Transparent pricing: line-item quotes with park fees and permits shown.
  • Honest seasons: we sell green-season value and talk you out of wrong months.
  • Slow where it counts: two-night stays, buffer days, no windscreen safaris.

Local expertise

We plan only Africa, and only with guides and camps we know personally. Driver-guides average a decade in their parks and are assigned by specialty: Migration positioning, photography, birding, family pacing. No generic reselling, no call centers.

Responsible tourism

Conservancy and community camps where bed-night fees fund rangers and schools (Mara conservancies, Khwai, Damaraland); fair Kilimanjaro crew wages to KPAP standards; capped group sizes on cultural visits; single-use plastic minimized on safari. Ask us where your money lands - we show you.

Community impact

Tourism income on our trips flows to school fees, supplies and community projects through the relationships below - plus the everyday impact of local hiring and community-camp bookings on every itinerary.

Shujaa wa Upendo Initiative

Mission: supporting vulnerable children, education and community projects. MAF Foundation

Adventure Safari Africa contributes 10% of profit to MAF Foundation. This figure is a configurable factual field (data/team.json - community.mafContributionPercent) and is stated identically wherever the relationship appears. No additional claims are made beyond this verified arrangement.

How tourism supports our community work

  1. Book: your safari's margin creates the giving pool.
  2. Allocate: 10% of profit to MAF Foundation plus direct project funding via Shujaa wa Upendo.
  3. Report: travelers receive plain-language impact notes - what was funded, where, with receipts on request.
