6-day Marangu itinerary

Day 1 - Marangu Gate to Mandara Hut (2,700m)

Rainforest hike, 4-5 hrs. Bunk beds, dining hut dinner.

Day 2 - Mandara to Horombo Hut (3,720m)

Moorland ascent with Mawenzi views, 5-6 hrs.

Day 3 - Acclimatization at Horombo

Day walk to Zebra Rocks or Mawenzi Hut base, return to sleep low. The day that saves summits.

Day 4 - Horombo to Kibo Hut (4,703m)

Alpine desert push, 5-6 hrs. Early dinner, midnight-ready rest.

Day 5 - Summit Uhuru (5,895m) to Horombo (3,720m)

Midnight start via Gilman's Point, long descent to Horombo.

Day 6 - Horombo to Marangu Gate, certificates

Descent, summit certificates, transfer to hotel.

5-day Marangu itinerary

Skips the Horombo acclimatization day: summit odds fall toward 65-70%, and the trip feels like a forced march above 4,000m. We sell it only to pre-acclimatized climbers - everyone else should take 6 days or switch to Lemosho.

Honest assessment

Marangu's huts suit rainy months and tent-averse trekkers, and it is the cheapest route. But ascent and descent share one trail (less scenery, more traffic), and the 5-day profile fails often. If budget allows 7 days, Machame beats 6-day Marangu on experience. Check the estimator.

Marangu FAQs

Why is Marangu success lowest?

Short profile plus no walk-high-sleep-low day on the 5-day format. The extra Horombo day on the 6-day version fixes most of it.

Are the huts comfortable?

Bunk beds with mattresses, solar light, dining huts with cooked meals - genuinely comfortable versus tents, and dry in rain.

5 or 6 days?

Six. The fifth-day saving costs summits. Operators pushing 5-day Marangu are selling failure rates.

What does it cost?

From about $2,600 for 6 days join-in. Cheapest on the mountain - verify crew wages behind bargain quotes.

Is Marangu good in rainy season?

Relatively: huts beat tents when it pours. Rongai is drier still - compare both for April/May/November.

How busy?

Very - the classic first-timer route. Start early from huts to walk ahead of groups.

Can beginners do it?

Yes on 6 days with training. But 7-day Machame or 8-day Lemosho serve beginners better for modest extra cost.

What gear?

Same as all routes - packing list. Hut nights need a lighter bag (still bring it - huts are unheated).

Safari after?

Yes - 5-day safari pairs perfectly from Arusha.
