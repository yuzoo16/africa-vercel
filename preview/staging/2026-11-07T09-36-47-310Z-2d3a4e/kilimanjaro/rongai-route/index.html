7-day Rongai itinerary

Day 1 - Rongai Gate to Simba Camp (2,625m)

Forest edge walk with colobus monkeys, 3-4 hrs. Quiet from step one.

Day 2 - Simba to Second Cave (3,450m)

Moorland ascent with Kibo opening ahead, 3-4 hrs.

Day 3 - Second Cave to Kikelewa (3,600m)

Gentle ridge walking toward Mawenzi (3-4 hrs) - the easy profile that defines Rongai.

Day 4 - Kikelewa to Mawenzi Tarn (4,330m)

Short steep pull to the stunning tarn camp beneath Mawenzi's spires.

Day 5 - Mawenzi Tarn to Kibo Hut (4,703m)

Cross the saddle to base camp; join the Marangu trail. Rest for summit night.

Day 6 - Summit Uhuru (5,895m) to Horombo (3,720m)

Midnight start via Gilman's Point; descend the Marangu side to Horombo.

Day 7 - Horombo to Marangu Gate, certificates

Final descent, summit certificates, transfer to hotel.

6-day Rongai itinerary

Combines the Second Cave and Kikelewa stages: fine for fit trekkers, but 7 days gives Mawenzi Tarn's acclimatization gift. Take 7 unless experienced at altitude.

Why Rongai

Northern slopes sit in Kilimanjaro's rain shadow - drier when southern routes soak (April, May, November). Gradients are the gentlest, crowds the thinnest, wildlife (colobus, buffalo, elephant signs) the best. Trade-off: less dramatic scenery variety than Lemosho and a Marangu-side descent. Rongai FAQs

Is Rongai the easiest route?

Gradient-wise, yes - but altitude cares about days, not steepness. Seven-day Rongai matches Machame odds; six-day versions converge downward.

Why is Rongai drier?

The northern side lies in the mountain's rain shadow. In April/May/November it can be merely damp while Machame pours.

6 or 7 days?

Seven for Mawenzi Tarn night and margin. Six only with prior altitude experience.

What does it cost?

From about $3,100 for 7 days join-in; remote northern logistics price slightly above Machame.

Will we see wildlife?

More than southern routes: colobus in the forest, buffalo and elephant sign on the moorland. Bring binoculars day one.

Descent route?

Down the Marangu side to Marangu Gate - meaning transfers start north (near Loitokitok) and end south. We handle the vehicle shuffle.

Good for beginners?

Excellent on 7 days: gentle gradients plus solitude suit nervous first-timers.

Best months?

Standard dry windows, plus April/May/November when it outperforms southern routes.

Safari after?

Yes - descend to Marangu side, straight into 5-day safari country.
