Kilimanjaro routes, compared honestly

The mountain is not the hard part - picking the right route is. These three cover most travellers, and we will tell you plainly if another one suits you better.

7 to 8 days · Scenic

Lemosho Route

The longest western approach, with the best acclimatisation profile and the highest summit success rate.

 6 to 7 days · Challenging

Machame Route

The Whiskey Route. Steeper and busier, but the scenery changes daily and the extra day earns its keep.

 5 to 6 days · Hut-based

Marangu Route

The classic dormitory-hut route and the shortest option - comfortable underfoot, but a lower success rate at five days.

Good to know

Start with the questions everyone asks

How much does an African safari cost?

Budget safaris run roughly $250-400 per person per day, mid-range $400-650, and luxury $700-1,200 or more. Park fees, a private 4×4 with guide, accommodation and most meals are usually included; international flights, visas and tips are not. Every proposal we send is priced line by line, so you can see exactly what you are paying for. See the full cost breakdown →.

When is the best time to go on safari?

There is no single best month - it depends on what you want to see. June to October is dry season across most of East and Southern Africa, with concentrated wildlife and easy road access. December to March brings the Migration calving season in the southern Serengeti. Gorilla trekking is possible year-round, though the drier months are more comfortable underfoot. Read the month-by-month guide →.

How many days do I need for a safari?

Five to seven days is the sweet spot for a single-country safari: it allows two or three parks without spending the whole trip driving. Add three or four days if you want a beach extension, a gorilla trek or a Kilimanjaro climb. Shorter three and four day itineraries work well if you fly between parks rather than drive.

Is a safari suitable for families with children?

Yes, with the right planning. We favour malaria-free reserves in South Africa for younger children, adjust game-drive lengths, and arrange interconnecting rooms or family tents. Minimum ages for gorilla and chimpanzee trekking are set by the parks themselves, so we build the itinerary around your youngest traveller. Plan a family safari →.

What is included in the price of a safari?

Your quote covers park and conservancy fees, accommodation, meals as stated, a private 4×4 safari vehicle with a professional guide, and drinking water throughout. Not included are international flights, entry visas, travel insurance, optional extras such as balloon flights, and gratuities. Nothing is added later without your approval.

How far in advance should I book?

Eight to twelve months ahead for peak season, gorilla permits and the best Migration camps, which sell out early. Three to six months is usually workable for green-season travel, when rates are lower and the plains are quiet and green. If you are travelling sooner than that, ask us anyway - we often have late availability.
