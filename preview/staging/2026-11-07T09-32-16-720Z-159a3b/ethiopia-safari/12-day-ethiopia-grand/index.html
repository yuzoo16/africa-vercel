Day-by-day

Days 1-3 - Lalibela

Two full days across all eleven rock churches with a specialist guide; Timkat ceremony if January. Fly in, no road days.

Day 4 - Gondar castles

Fasil Ghebbi castles, Debre Berhan church murals; fly Gondar.

Days 5-7 - Simien trek

Sankaber to Geech: gelada troops, lammergeiers, Jinbar Falls. Mules carry gear; acclimatisation day built in.

Day 8 - Fly south: Arba Minch

Addis connection, Dorze village, Lake Chamo boat.

Days 9-10 - Turmi and Hamar country

Villages, Evangadi dancing, seasonal bull-jumping, markets.

Day 11 - Karo and Mursi

Riverside body painting, Mago park Mursi villages, Jinka overnight.

Day 12 - Fly Addis, farewell

Morning flight, day room, farewell dinner, evening departures.

FAQs

North first or Omo first?

North first: altitude acclimatisation helps, and cultural intensity lands better after historic context. Flights make either order possible.

How do festival dates affect this?

Timkat (Jan 19) transforms Lalibela; Meskel (Sep 27) lights Gondar. Festival weeks need 6-plus months booking and premium pricing.

Is 12 days rushed?

No - flights do the heavy lifting. Road-only versions need 16-plus days for the same ground.

What fitness is needed?

Moderate hill fitness for Simien; basic mobility elsewhere. Altitude and heat are managed by pacing, not heroics.

Can we add Danakil?

Yes for the hardy: plus 3-4 days expedition. Ask about current conditions and seasonal timing.

Family suitable?

Teens-plus for the full arc; under-12s better on north-only formats. Omo intensity and drives challenge young kids.
