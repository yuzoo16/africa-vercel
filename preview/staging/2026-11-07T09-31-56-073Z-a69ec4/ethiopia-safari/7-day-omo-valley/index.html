Fly Addis to Jinka or Arba Minch, then loop Turmi, Omorate and the Mago/Omo parks: Hamar villages and (seasonal) bull-jumping ceremonies, Mursi lip-plate culture, Karo riverside body painting, plus Dassanech and Konso highland terraces. Market days (Turmi Monday, Dimeka Tuesday/Saturday, Jinka) anchor the routing.

Day-by-day

Day 1 - Addis arrival and briefing

Morning: land Addis. Midday: cultural briefing and protocols session. Afternoon: National Museum (Lucy) and market walk. Evening: traditional dinner with dance performance.

Day 2 - Fly south: Arba Minch and Dorze

Morning flight; Dorze highland village (bamboo huts, weaving, false-banana food); Lake Chamo crocodile-market boat; overnight Arba Minch.

Days 3-4 - Turmi and Hamar country

Hamar village visits, Evangadi night dancing, seasonal bull-jumping ceremony; Turmi Monday market. Overnights Turmi lodge/camp.

Day 5 - Omorate and the Omo River

Cross to Dassanech country by local boat; riverside villages; return via Karo for sunset body-painting photography (agreed fees).

Day 6 - Mursi and Jinka

Mago park to Mursi villages with community guide; Jinka museum and Ari villages; overnight Jinka.

Day 7 - Fly Addis, farewell

Morning flight; day room, souvenir time, farewell dinner; evening departures.

FAQs

Is bull-jumping guaranteed?

No - ceremonies follow family calendars, mostly harvest season. We time trips to market-plus-ceremony windows and confirm locally before you fly.

Photography rules?

Always ask via your guide; agreed per-photo fees are normal and fair. Some villages and ceremonies restrict cameras - respect it instantly.

How rustic is it?

Lodges in Turmi/Jinka are comfortable; remote nights are tented camps with bucket showers. Roads are rough - 4x4s, patience, dust.

Is it suitable for kids?

Teens-plus generally; long drives and cultural intensity challenge under-12s. Ask for family pacing.

What fitness is needed?

Basic mobility: village walks, heat, uneven ground. No trekking required.

Can we combine with the north?

Yes - 12 days north plus Omo by domestic flights. See the grand tour.
