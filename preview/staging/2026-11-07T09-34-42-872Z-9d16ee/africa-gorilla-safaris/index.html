FAQs

Uganda or Rwanda for gorillas?

Uganda for cost and combinations; Rwanda for speed and luxury lodges. Most first-timers choose Uganda.

One number settles it for most: the US$700 permit difference equals two extra lodge nights in Uganda or one Kibale chimp permit plus a Queen Elizabeth day. Choose Rwanda if your trip is under five days or you fly in and out of Kigali anyway; choose Uganda if you have a week and want the trek to sit inside a real safari.

How fit must we be?

Moderate - one to five hours on steep slopes. Porters carry packs and steady you; take one.

Minimum age?

Fifteen, strictly enforced in both countries.

How do we choose between the options above?

Match the country to your priority: Uganda gorilla trekking: Bwindi + Mgahinga, permits handled. Uganda wildlife combos: Chimps, lions, Nile falls. 3-day fly-in trek: Permits-first long weekend. 7-day primates + savanna: The complete week. Tell us your month and party and a specialist narrows it to one route.

What do the guide prices on this page include?

Guide prices such as 3 days (Gorillas only, fly-in) - From $2,650; 5-7 days (Plus chimps and savanna) - From $3,200; 10-14 days (Grand tour) - From $5,800 are mid-range baselines per person (two travelers), including private vehicle and guide, accommodation, park fees and listed transfers. Peak season and luxury tiers quote higher - every proposal is line-itemed.

Those per-day figures assume two travelers sharing one vehicle in green season; every additional passenger splits the same vehicle bill, and peak-season park tariffs can lift a day by US$30-80 before any upgrade. Treat them as the floor of a mid-range build, not as a ceiling - every proposal we issue line-items the same structure.

What photography gear should we bring?

A 100 to 400mm zoom covers 90 percent of sightings; add a wide second body for landscapes. Beanbags beat tripods in vehicles. We provide charging setups and dust management briefings - tell us your kit and targets.

Are your guides qualified?

Our driver-guides average a decade in their parks, hold national guiding licenses plus first-aid certification, and are assigned by specialty - Migration positioning, photography, birding or family pacing.

Can older travelers or limited mobility join?

Often yes: game drives are vehicle-based, many lodges have ground-floor rooms with walk-in showers, and we grade walking and trekking honestly. Tell us constraints and we design around them - except gorilla trekking, which demands real hill fitness.

How far ahead should we book?

Peak Migration and gorilla-permit months need 6 to 12 months. Green season often works with 2 to 4 months notice.
