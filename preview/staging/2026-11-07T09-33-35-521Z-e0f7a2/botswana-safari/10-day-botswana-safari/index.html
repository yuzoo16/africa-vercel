Why this 10-day Botswana safari itinerary

10 days on the Botswana circuit: Okavango Delta, Moremi / Khwai, Chobe, Victoria Falls. Every day below is a real day on the ground, not a transfer with a game drive attached.

Why Okavango Delta comes first

The flood arrives when the surrounding country is driest, so the delta is the ecological centre of the trip - and that is where the route opens.

Victoria Falls gets 5 nights

Victoria Falls holds 5 of the 10 days, and that is deliberate: the falls need time for both the wet-season spray and the dry-season rock face, plus the activities around them.

What 10 days buys

10 days moves past the point of rushing: whole days rather than stops, and enough slack that weather or wildlife can bend the plan without breaking it.

Getting around Botswana

Botswana moves by light aircraft between camps, because the road distances between the delta and the Chobe riverfront would eat the safari. The delta itself is boat and mokoro rather than road, and the concessions around Khwai and Moremi allow night drives and off-road tracking that the national parks do not.

Best time to book

Botswana runs on two seasons. The one you pick changes what you see, what the camps cost and how far ahead you need to commit.

Dry season

May to October is the dry season and the classic window: animals concentrate on the permanent water, the delta flood arrives from the north, and the bush is at its most open. It is also the high season for both price and camps.

Green season

November to April is the green season, when the rains transform the pans, the birding peaks and the newborn animals arrive. Rates fall substantially and the parks are close to empty.

What actually drives the timing

The Okavango flood is the exception to every seasonal rule: it arrives from Angola during the dry months, so the delta is fullest from June to August precisely when the surrounding country is driest. Makgadikgadi works the other way, with the summer rains turning the pans green from December.

How far ahead to book

June to August and the Christmas window want six to twelve months of notice, because delta camps are small and fly-in seat counts are fixed. Green-season Botswana often works on two to four months.

FAQs

What happens if it rains?

Game drives run rain or shine - vehicles have roofs and ponchos, animals remain active, and green-season light is superb for photography. Only true downpours pause activity, and camps flex schedules around them.

What insurance do we need?

Comprehensive travel insurance is required, including medical evacuation (AMREF/Flying Doctors cover, about 30 dollars, is recommended for remote circuits). Bring policy details - camps and operators ask for them.

Can we customise the route?

Freely - add nights, upgrade camps, swap parks or append beach and trekking. Request a proposal and we re-quote line by line.

Do we need a visa?

Most visitors need one: Tanzania offers visas on arrival for many nationalities, Kenya uses an eTA applied for online before travel, Uganda an e-visa. We confirm the current rule for your passport before you fly.

One decision per country, and they differ. Tanzania sells visas on arrival for most passports - US$50, US$100 for United States citizens - or by eVisa in advance. Kenya has issued only the eTA since 2024, at US$32.50 online at least three days before departure. Uganda runs an e-visa printed before you fly, and the US$100 East African Tourist Visa folds Kenya, Uganda and Rwanda into a single stamp. Six months of passport validity covers all three. Send us passport details at proposal stage and we confirm every rule for your nationality before you commit to flights.

Traveling solo - how do single supplements work?

All itineraries run solo. Expect a 20 to 50 percent supplement where lodges charge per room; vehicles cost the same whether one or six ride. Ask for the honest supplement math per trip.

Is a balloon flight worth adding?

Once, yes: a dawn flight over the Serengeti or Mara with a bush breakfast runs 550 to 600 dollars and minimum ages apply. Book at proposal stage - seats sell out months ahead in season.

How far ahead should we book?

Peak Migration and gorilla-permit months need 6 to 12 months. Green season often works with 2 to 4 months notice.

Is this trip suitable for children?

With a private vehicle and pools at stops, yes for most routes. Gorilla trekking has a strict minimum age of 15.

How much should we tip?

As a guideline: 15 to 25 dollars per day for your driver-guide (per group, not per person), 10 to 15 dollars per day into camp communal tip boxes, and 20 to 30 dollars for a gorilla porter. Restaurants 10 percent where service is not included.
