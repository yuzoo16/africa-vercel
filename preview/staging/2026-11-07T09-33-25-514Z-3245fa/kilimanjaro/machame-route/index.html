7-day Machame itinerary

Day 1 - Machame Gate to Machame Camp (3,010m)

Rainforest ascent, 5-7 hrs. Camp briefing and early night.

Day 2 - Machame to Shira Cave (3,840m)

Steep moorland climb out of the forest (4-6 hrs). Kibo reveals.

Day 3 - Shira to Barranco via Lava Tower (4,630m)

Walk-high-sleep-low to 4,630m, descend to Barranco (3,976m). Key acclimatization day.

Day 4 - Barranco to Karanga (4,035m)

Barranco Wall scramble, short valley afternoon for recovery.

Day 5 - Karanga to Barafu (4,673m)

Morning push to base camp; rest, prep, sleep by 7pm.

Day 6 - Summit Uhuru (5,895m) to Mweka (3,100m)

Midnight start, Stella at sunrise, Uhuru after; long descent to Mweka.

Day 7 - Mweka Gate exit, certificates, transfer

Forest descent, summit certificates, Arusha/Moshi hotel.

6-day Machame itinerary

Skips Karanga: Barranco to Barafu in one long day (8-9 hrs). For fit, experienced trekkers only - success drops toward 78%. Most climbers should take 7.

Machame vs Lemosho

Same summit, different start: Machame is steeper on days 1-2, busier throughout, slightly cheaper (one fewer day). Lemosho wins on scenery variety and success rate. Choose Machame for the classic social trail; Lemosho for the best odds. Compare: Lemosho | Lemosho vs Machame article | estimator

Machame FAQs

Is Machame harder than Lemosho?

Slightly: steeper early days and one fewer acclimatization day on the 6-day format. The 7-day Machame closes most of the gap.

How busy is Machame?

The busiest route - its popularity is the main drawback. Start times and camp choices mitigate it; Rongai is the quiet alternative.

6 or 7 days?

Seven, without debate for first-timers. Six suits fit trekkers with prior altitude nights.

What does it cost?

Quality 7-day climbs from about $2,950 join-in. Private and luxury tiers higher.

Best months?

January-March and June-October. Shoulder months trade some rain risk for solitude.

Is the Barranco Wall scary?

It looks worse than it is: a 1-2 hour scramble with brief exposure, guides spotting every step. No technical gear needed.

Can beginners do Machame?

Yes on 7 days with hill training. Summit night is long - mental preparation matters as much as legs.

What gear matters most?

Boots, bag, layers, headlamp - see the packing list. Rent the bag and poles in Arusha if flying light.

Safari after?

Yes - 5-day safari is the classic recovery pairing.
