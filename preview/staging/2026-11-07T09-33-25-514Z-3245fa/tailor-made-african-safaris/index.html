What is a tailor-made safari?

A tailor-made safari is built around the way you want to travel, not around a fixed itinerary that every traveler follows.

Your travel dates, interests, preferred destinations, accommodation style, budget, pace and the people travelling with you all influence how the safari is designed. Tanzania's official tourism guidance makes the same point: safaris can be tailor-made according to travelers' preferences, timing and interests, and a private vehicle gives you greater choice over the type and duration of your game drives.

A properly planned tailor-made safari considers where you should go, how long you should stay, how the destinations connect, when you should travel, how you move between them, what you want to experience and how the overall journey should feel.

Whether you are planning a first African safari, a honeymoon, a family holiday, a photography trip, a migration safari, a luxury journey or a safari followed by Zanzibar, the process starts with what matters to you. How planning works → Tailor-made or fixed → Safari FAQs →

Why tailor-made instead of a fixed package

Tailor-made does not mean taking a standard package and adding or removing a lodge.

A fixed package is built once and sold many times. That makes it efficient, and for some trips it is exactly the right answer. It also assumes the same priorities for everyone who books it: the same number of nights, the same parks, the same driving days, the same standard of accommodation and the same start date.

A tailor-made safari works the other way round. The route is built after we understand what the trip is for, and every part of it is chosen because it serves your priorities rather than because it fits a template.

  • The route follows your priorities instead of a standard loop
  • Nights are allocated where the wildlife or the landscape is strongest for your dates
  • Driving days are set to the pace you actually enjoy
  • Accommodation is chosen for the section of route it serves
  • The budget is spent where it changes the experience

None of this changes the destinations themselves. The Serengeti is the Serengeti in either case. What changes is when you reach it, how long you stay and what the rest of the journey looks like. The planning process → Compare the two approaches →

1. Start with your safari idea

You do not need to know exactly where you want to go before you contact us.

You might simply tell us:

  • "We have 8 days, we want to see the Great Migration and we would like a few days on the beach."
  • "We are a family with two children and want a comfortable safari without spending the whole day driving."
  • "We are photographers and want enough time in the Serengeti to stay with wildlife sightings."

That is enough to begin. We turn those first ideas into the questions that determine the right route.

We look at:

  • Travel dates
  • Number of travelers
  • Ages of children
  • Arrival and departure points
  • Main wildlife interests
  • Preferred countries
  • Accommodation level
  • Approximate budget
  • Safari duration
  • Preferred pace
  • Photography interests
  • Cultural experiences
  • Beach requirements
  • Special occasions
  • Dietary requirements
  • Mobility or accessibility considerations

The more we understand about your trip, the more precisely the itinerary can be designed. What matters most → What we need from you →

2. We understand what matters most

Not every traveler wants the same safari.

Two people travelling to Tanzania during the same month could need completely different itineraries. One may want:

  • Maximum wildlife time
  • Comfortable lodges and shorter driving days
  • Photography and longer game drives
  • Family-friendly pacing
  • Safari and Zanzibar

This is why we do not start by sending the same itinerary to everyone. Your priorities determine the route.

  • If wildlife is your main priority, we can allocate more nights to the strongest wildlife areas.
  • If comfort is what matters, we can reduce long road transfers and consider flights between destinations.
  • If budget is the main constraint, we can adjust accommodation and transport without automatically removing the destinations that matter most.
  • If you are travelling with children, the itinerary can be structured around shorter transfers, appropriate accommodation and a more manageable daily rhythm.

4. We build the route around geography

A beautiful list of destinations does not automatically make a good safari itinerary. The destinations need to connect logically.

For example, in northern Tanzania, a route might follow:

  • Northern circuit by road - Arusha → Tarangire → Ngorongoro → Serengeti → Arusha.

That keeps the journey moving in one direction instead of repeatedly crossing the same territory. For a longer safari, flights may make more sense than spending an entire day on the road, because a route such as:

  • Safari then coast by air - Arusha → Serengeti → Zanzibar.

can be structured very differently from a safari that returns by road to Arusha before continuing to the coast. The purpose is not simply to visit more places. It is to make the journey between places work as part of the safari. Match the route to the season → Choose how you travel →

5. We match the itinerary to the season

The same destination can offer a different experience depending on when you travel, so your dates influence the route.

If your priority is the Great Migration, the itinerary needs to be planned around the seasonal movement of the herds rather than simply adding "Serengeti" to the itinerary. If your priority is general wildlife viewing, the best route may be different.

Season also affects:

  • Accommodation availability
  • Prices
  • Road conditions
  • Wildlife concentrations
  • Landscapes
  • Birdlife
  • Beach conditions
  • Visitor numbers

Tanzania's tourism authority recommends booking well in advance for peak travel periods, particularly June to October and the Christmas and New Year period. A tailor-made safari therefore begins with a single question: what do you want to experience during the dates you have available? How far ahead to plan →

6. We decide how you should move between destinations

Transportation is one of the biggest decisions in safari planning, and it is made per section of the route rather than once for the whole trip.

Depending on the itinerary, you may travel by:

Private 4x4 safari vehicle

Best when you want:

  • A private guide
  • Flexible game drives
  • Overland exploration
  • More control over your daily schedule

Domestic flight

Useful when:

  • Distances are long
  • Safari time is limited
  • You want to avoid lengthy road transfers
  • You are connecting remote safari areas

Scheduled flight

Can be appropriate when the route and the departure schedule fit your itinerary. Scheduled services are cheaper than private charters, but they fix the times you have to be at the airstrip.

Road transfer

Often makes sense for shorter distances, or when the journey itself forms part of the experience rather than something to get through as quickly as possible.

Combination

Many tailor-made safaris use a mixture of road and air, because the goal is not to use the same transport method everywhere. It is to choose the method that makes sense for each section of the journey. Match the accommodation → We check the logistics →

7. We choose accommodation for the route

Accommodation should support the itinerary.

We consider:

  • Location
  • Access to the park
  • Distance from attractions
  • Accommodation style
  • Room configuration
  • Facilities
  • Meal arrangements
  • Family requirements
  • Privacy
  • Views
  • Activities available
  • Your budget

A lodge located closer to the area you want to explore may be more useful than a more luxurious property that requires longer transfers each day. This is particularly important in large safari destinations, where a beautiful property in the wrong corner of the park costs you game-drive hours.

A tailor-made itinerary therefore does not ask which lodge looks nicest. It asks which accommodation works best for this part of the safari. Match the budget → Design the pace →

Mid-range

9. We design the pace

This is one of the most overlooked parts of safari planning. A seven-day safari does not need to mean seven days of constantly moving between destinations.

You can design the trip around:

Slow safari

Stay several nights in fewer locations. Best for:

  • Couples
  • Families
  • Photographers
  • Travelers wanting relaxation

Classic safari

Move between several major wildlife areas. Best for:

  • First-time safari travelers
  • Travelers wanting variety
  • Shorter trips

Intensive safari

Cover more destinations within a limited number of days. Best for:

  • Travelers with limited vacation time
  • Travelers who want to see several ecosystems

Fly-in safari

Use flights to reduce road transfers and spend more time in remote safari areas.

The correct pace depends on the traveler, and it is easier to change on paper than once the trip has started. Build in the experiences →

Does tailor-made mean private?

Not necessarily.

A tailor-made itinerary describes how the trip is designed, while a private safari describes how the safari is operated. You can have a tailor-made safari that is private, or a tailor-made itinerary built from selected shared components.

For example, you might want a private safari vehicle in Tanzania but use a scheduled flight to Zanzibar and stay at a standard beach resort. The two concepts should not be confused. Private safaris explained → Group safaris explained →

Does tailor-made mean luxury?

No.

A tailor-made safari can be budget, mid-range, premium or luxury. Customization is about matching the trip to the traveler, not automatically increasing the price.

A traveler might prefer to spend more on additional Serengeti nights rather than upgrading every hotel. Another traveler may prefer fewer nights but luxury accommodation. The itinerary can be structured accordingly. How budget works → How pace works →

How far in advance should you plan?

The ideal planning window depends on:

  • Travel season
  • Destination
  • Accommodation
  • Group size
  • Special activities
  • Flights
  • Migration timing
  • Holiday periods

Peak travel periods can require earlier planning because accommodation and flights become more limited. Tanzania's tourism guidance recommends booking as far ahead as possible for peak periods.

For a simple off-season itinerary there may be more flexibility. For a honeymoon, a migration safari, a family holiday or a complex multi-country itinerary, earlier planning generally gives you more options. What we need from you → Changing your mind →

What happens if you change your mind?

That is part of the process.

You may start with "we want a 7-day safari" and then realize you would rather have 5 days of safari and 4 days in Zanzibar.

Or you may decide you would rather spend less on accommodation and add another night in the Serengeti.

A tailor-made itinerary can be adjusted while it is still being planned. The objective is to arrive at a journey that makes sense for you, not to force your preferences into a pre-existing package. How we refine →

The difference is in the details

A tailor-made African safari is not simply a standard itinerary with your name added to the top. It is the process of connecting your dates, interests, destinations, route, accommodation, transport, activities, budget and pace into one coherent journey.

  • You may want the Great Migration but not want to spend every night changing camps.
  • You may want luxury accommodation but prefer to invest more of your budget in time inside the Serengeti.
  • You may want a family safari without long daily drives.
  • You may want a private honeymoon safari followed by Zanzibar.
  • Or you may simply know that you want to see Africa and need help deciding where to begin.

Tailor-made safari FAQs

What is a tailor-made safari?

A tailor-made safari is an itinerary designed around the traveler's dates, interests, budget, accommodation preferences, destinations and preferred pace rather than a fixed package.

Can I choose my own safari destinations?

Yes. You can request specific destinations, or you can tell us what you want to experience and we can recommend destinations that fit those priorities.

Can I change the itinerary after receiving the proposal?

Yes. The planning stage is where you can adjust destinations, nights, accommodation, transport and activities before the safari is finalized.

Can I combine several African countries?

Yes. Multi-country journeys can be designed where the geography, flights and available time make the combination practical.

Can I combine a tailor-made safari with Zanzibar?

Yes. Tanzania safari itineraries can be designed to continue to Zanzibar for a beach extension.

Is a tailor-made safari more expensive?

Not automatically. The cost depends on destinations, accommodation, transport, season, activities and trip length. Tailor-made planning can also help allocate your budget toward the experiences that matter most.

Not automatically. A tailor-made itinerary carries the same operator margin as a published route; what changes is where the money goes - nights in Migration zones, private guiding, or extra internal flights rather than standard lodge categories. The premium shows up only if you upgrade the components; the design work itself is usually worth the flexibility, not a fixed surcharge.

Can I have a private vehicle?

Yes, where available. A private vehicle gives you greater control over the pace and game-drive schedule than a shared group safari.

Can I fly between safari destinations?

Yes. Domestic flights can be incorporated where they make the itinerary more efficient.

Can I plan a safari around the Great Migration?

Yes. The itinerary can be designed around the expected seasonal movement of the herds, although wildlife movements are natural events and exact sightings cannot be guaranteed.

Can you plan a family safari?

Yes. The itinerary can take children's ages, accommodation, driving distances, activities and daily pacing into account.

Can you plan a honeymoon safari?

Yes. A honeymoon itinerary can combine private game drives, carefully selected accommodation, romantic experiences and a beach extension such as Zanzibar.

How long does a tailor-made safari need to be?

There is no fixed minimum. A short three-day safari can be tailor-made, just as a multi-week African journey can be customized.
